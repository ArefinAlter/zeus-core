# Code of Conduct

Zeus follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is published at that address and is incorporated here by reference.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the work, and do not harass anyone.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or contributors that violate these expectations.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub:
open a private [security advisory](https://github.com/ArefinAlter/zeus-core/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct report"
(it is visible only to the maintainers), or contact a maintainer listed in the repository's commit history.
Reports are handled confidentially.
